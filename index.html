HOW TO PLAY

You get exactly one bullet. Aim with your mouse — a dotted line shows roughly where it will bounce off the walls.

Left-click to fire. The bullet travels fast, ricochets off walls, and keeps flying after it kills an enemy. Clear every enemy in the room with that single shot to win it.

NORMAL RUN — clear all 10 rooms back to back. One miss sends you all the way back to room 1.

PRACTICE — replay the same room as many times as you like, no pressure, no timer.
